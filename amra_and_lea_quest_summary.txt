QUEST STORYLINE:
Based on extracted dialogues, the story involves:

1. **The Romance**: Amra and Lea were in love, but Lea's father (a wealthy man) 
   disapproved and sent Amra away. A rich magician was also courting Lea.

2. **The Gift**: Before leaving, Lea gave Amra her most valuable possession - 
   "das Pfand der Götter" (the Pledge of the Gods), a golden ring she received 
   from the goddess Elen herself.

3. **The Search**: Amra went on a quest to find Lea, taking the ring with him.
   He was accompanied by warrior brothers including Tyrgar (a fisherman) and 
   Craig.

4. **The Tragedy**: In the desert, they encountered a powerful dark magician 
   who demanded the ring. Amra fought bravely but was killed. His companions 
   buried him with his weapons and armor.

5. **The Aftermath**: Lea disappeared, possibly buried in Wisper. Various 
   NPCs hold pieces of this story and guide the player to find Amra's armor.

KEY LOCATIONS:
- Liannon (city where Sunder the blacksmith lives)
- Wildland Pass (meeting location)
- Wisper (where Lea's supposed grave is)
- Desert location (where Amra died)
